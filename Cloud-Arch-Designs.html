Strategic Considerations

SEBI Cloud Adoption & Localization

The Consideration: Financial data must reside within Indian jurisdictions. If expanding to the DIFC (Middle East), local GCC data sovereignty laws apply. The architecture must guarantee isolation of client data in multi-tenant cloud environments.

Cybersecurity & Cyber Resilience (CSCRF)

The Consideration: Following the CISO vacuum, GFSL must adhere to SEBI's updated August 2024 CSCRF guidelines. Any cloud deployment must natively support Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing (VAPT), strict asset management, and automated incident response.

📄 Read SEBI August 2024 CSCRF Circular

Data Gravity & Egress Costs

The Consideration: "Data Gravity" dictates that applications should be deployed as close to their databases as possible. Cloud providers charge heavily for "egress"—moving data out of the cloud. If SPARC (Back Office) processes massive ledger files that must be continuously pulled down to 500+ physical branches, placing the database in the cloud will cause cost overruns.

Microsecond Latency Limits

The Consideration: High-frequency trading systems (like FLIP) cannot tolerate the network latency introduced by routing traffic to a public cloud data center and back. For these specific workloads, the speed of light dictates they must remain on-premises or at an edge colocation facility next to the exchange.

The a16z Repatriation Math (TCO vs. COR)

The Consideration: Not all workloads are cheaper in the cloud. If steady-state, predictable workloads push the cloud bill to more than 50% of the Cost of Revenue (COR), the leadership should consider a hybrid model to preserve gross margins, treating infrastructure as a measurable business KPI.
